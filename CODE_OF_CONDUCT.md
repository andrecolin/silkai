# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticize code, not
people. This is a project about GPU scheduling, not a place to work out
anything else.

## Expected behaviour

- Give useful, specific feedback on the work.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Respect that maintainer time is volunteered, and that "no" is a complete
  answer to a feature request.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about who someone is, rather than what they wrote.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussion.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer at <andrecolin@outlook.com>. Reports stay
private. The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, close issues and
pull requests, or block accounts, and does not owe a public explanation for
doing so.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to fit a project this size.
